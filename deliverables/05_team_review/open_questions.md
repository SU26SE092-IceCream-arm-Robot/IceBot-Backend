# Open Questions Register — IceBot Backend Deliverables

**Purpose**: Consolidate unresolved questions already present or directly derived from labeled uncertainty in the Project Introduction, SRS, RTM (Requirements Traceability Matrix), UML, database design, and existing review notes. This file does not resolve them or introduce new features.

**Use**: During triage, classify every entry as **Factual verification**, **Code/docs discrepancy**, **Product decision**, **Architecture decision**, **Operational requirement gap**, **Report presentation decision**, or **Roadmap question**. Assign severity (Blocker/Major/Minor/Editorial), owner, due date, blocking status, dependencies, and status (Open/Needs Evidence/Proposed/Accepted/Rejected/Deferred/Duplicate/Not Applicable/Fixed/Verified). `[Open Question]` is retained here until triage; known factual corrections belong in the change log rather than being treated as optional decisions.
